# Community conduct / コミュニティの行動規範

Be respectful and specific. Critique the game, evidence, or proposal, not the person. Harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing, repeated unsolicited promotion, and impersonating NASA or another organization are not allowed. Avoid posting private information or treating this game as real medical, flight, or emergency instruction.

Moderators may edit labels, hide or remove harmful content, and restrict participation as needed. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub's report features. Public bug and discussion threads are for game feedback, not private incident reports.

具体的で敬意ある議論をお願いします。人ではなく提案や根拠を検討してください。嫌がらせ、差別、脅迫、個人情報の公開、宣伝の連投、NASA等のなりすましは禁止します。問題のある投稿は管理者が非表示・削除・参加制限を行うことがあります。
