## English page text

**Play free in your browser.** Astronaut Academy is GatsbyZero's independent educational game about astronaut training. Nine courses cover Earth orbit, ISS systems, space medicine, EVA, robotics, flight and survival, emergency response, lunar geology, and mission control. Each course includes 10 lessons with primary-source links, 16 quiz questions, and an interactive 3D exercise. Switch between English and Japanese without losing local progress.

Start with Orientation. Use the mouse or touch to select buttons and 3D markers; keyboard navigation is also available. Your progress is saved in this browser, without an account. In-game scores and certificates are fictional learning achievements, not NASA qualifications. Exercise timings and thresholds are educational game rules, not real operational instructions.

**Help improve it:** [Play feedback, content and source corrections, ideas, and translation](https://github.com/Gatsby007max/astronaut-academy/discussions). [Report a reproducible bug](https://github.com/Gatsby007max/astronaut-academy/issues/new/choose). [Read the source and contribute](https://github.com/Gatsby007max/astronaut-academy).

This is a free, independent project. NASA did not create, certify, endorse, or partner on it. NASA media and sources used inside the educational game are individually credited in the [asset register](https://github.com/Gatsby007max/astronaut-academy/blob/main/ASSET-CREDITS.md) and in the game. The cover and T-38 scene render contain no NASA media. The robotics screenshot includes a credited NASA SVS star map in the background.

English translation drafts, parts of the code, and the original vector cover were made with Codex assistance and are open to correction. In itch.io's AI disclosure, select **Yes**, **Text & Dialog**, **Code**, and **Graphics**. The cover's provenance is recorded in [ASSET-CREDITS.md](../ASSET-CREDITS.md).
